Rack It Out Safely

A short safety video for qualified electrical workers who rack low-voltage draw-out circuit breakers. It covers the three checks before racking, using a remote racking device, and verifying the breaker position before lockout.

Run time3:30
AudienceQualified electrical workers
FormatLive action + motion graphics
NarrationOff-camera VO, ~140 wpm
VO voiceoverOST on-screen textSFX sound effectCU / MS / WS close-up, medium, wide shot
TimeVisualAudio
Scene 1 · Cold open
0:00
WS: switchgear room. A technician in arc-rated PPE stands at a breaker compartment. Freeze frame.
OST: Racking is one of the highest-risk tasks in the electrical room
VORacking a breaker takes about a minute. If something goes wrong during that minute, it happens inches from your face.SFX: low room hum under VO
0:12
Title card over slow push-in on the breaker door.
Rack It Out Safely
VOIn the next three minutes, you'll see how to rack a low-voltage breaker without standing in front of it.
Scene 2 · Three checks before you touch it
0:22
MS: technician reviews a work order and energized work permit on a clipboard.
Check 1: Authorization
VOFirst, authorization. Confirm the work order, the breaker number, and any permit your site requires. Racking the wrong breaker is a real and common mistake.
0:38
CU: arc flash label on the gear. Motion graphic circles incident energy, PPE category, and arc flash boundary.
Check 2: Read the label
VOSecond, read the arc flash label. It tells you the incident energy, the PPE you need, and the arc flash boundary. That boundary is where you want to be standing when the breaker moves.
0:56
MS: technician dons arc-rated hood, gloves, and face shield. Quick cuts of each item.
Check 3: PPE on before the door opens
VOThird, put on your PPE before you open anything. Match it to the label, and inspect it. A torn glove or a scratched face shield doesn't protect you.
Scene 3 · Open the breaker
1:12
CU: technician presses the OPEN button. CU on the indicator flag changing to OPEN.
Verify OPEN on the indicator
VOOpen the breaker and look at the indicator. Don't rely on the button. The interlock is there to stop you from racking a closed breaker, but your eyes are the first check.
1:28
Motion graphic: cutaway of the breaker showing closing springs. Text callout points to the manufacturer manual.
Follow the manufacturer's racking procedure
VOSome breakers also need their closing springs discharged before racking. Every manufacturer does this a little differently, so follow the instructions for the gear in front of you.
Scene 4 · Rack remotely
1:44
MS: technician attaches a remote racking device to the racking port, then runs its cable back across the room.
Use the remote racking device
VOAttach the remote racking device to the racking port. Then walk the controller back, outside the arc flash boundary.
2:00
WS from behind the technician: floor marking shows the boundary. Technician stands well outside it and presses the controller.
Stand outside the arc flash boundary
VODistance is your best protection. With a remote racker, the breaker moves and you don't have to be there. Keep other workers out of the area, too.SFX: steady racking motor whir
2:18
Motion graphic: three-position diagram (Connected, Test, Disconnected). A marker slides to Disconnected.
Connected → Test → Disconnected
VOThe breaker travels from Connected, through Test, to Disconnected. In Test, control power is connected but the main contacts are separated from the bus. Stop at Disconnected.
Scene 5 · Verify and lock out
2:36
CU: position indicator reads DISCONNECTED. Technician removes the racking device.
Confirm the position indicator
VOConfirm the position on the indicator before you approach. If it doesn't match what you expect, stop and call your supervisor.
2:50
CU: technician applies a lock and tag to the breaker compartment and shutters per site LOTO procedure.
Lock and tag per your site LOTO procedure
VOApply your lock and tag according to your site's lockout program. Racked out is not the same as safe. Lockout is what keeps it that way.
Scene 6 · Recap
3:06
Recap card builds one line at a time over the gear in soft focus.
Authorize · Read the label · PPE · Open and verify · Rack remotely · Verify and lock out
VOAuthorize. Read the label. Suit up. Open and verify. Rack from a distance. Then verify and lock out. Every time, on every breaker.
3:24
Fade to black. End slate.
Questions? Talk to your electrical safety lead.
SFX: room hum fades out

Production notes

  • Shoot on de-energized training gear only. Every on-screen worker wears PPE that matches the label shown.
  • Have the site's electrical safety SME review the final cut against the site electrical safety program and the manufacturer's instructions.
  • Caption all narration. On-screen text stays up for at least three seconds.
